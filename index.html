Good to know

Quick answers.

Where are you?

Unit 9, Liberty Corner, James Joyce Street, Dublin 1, D01 K7N1. We’re a 2-minute walk from Connolly Station.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Saturday 09:15 to 19:30, and Sunday 11:00 to 17:00.

What services do you offer?

Haircuts, beard trims, eyebrow shaping and hot towel shaves.

How do I book?

Tap “Book your chair”, choose your barber, service and time. You can also call us on 083 088 2425.

Do you take cards?

Yes, we accept both card and cash.

Is the shop accessible, and where can I park?

The shop is wheelchair accessible and has free WiFi. There is paid parking nearby.
